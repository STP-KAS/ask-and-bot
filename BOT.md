## Watch

- The master file's sentence on spendable layer-1 stables, and the KCC-20 status. Draft, and "no spendable L1 stable", means the gate in plan.md is shut. Say that in one line when someone asks if a hunt can run.
- A transaction that would open the gate. POC opens when a stranger can receive a coin that locks collateral, redeems, spends promotional balances first, refuses to redeem those promotional balances, and has no admin freeze. Tether opens when a stranger can receive an issuer coin whose freeze leaves KAS and leaves the POC-style coin. Point at the txid, the amount, and the commit. A thread that announces a stable stays on the reading list.
- The square. A tKAS shop payment or a bank lock lands on the reserve as a Testnet-10 transaction. A short payment is refused. The same transaction does not mint twice. POCencept and KUSDT balances move only in the ledger. A KUSDT freeze leaves POCencept and tKAS spendable. Redeem returns tKAS only for the locked part. A failed redeem puts the toy balance back. The practice purse spends first and does not redeem.
- The fee. A Testnet-10 send pays twice the ordinary quote, and never under 200 sompi per gram. The faucet sentence stays 0.6 tKAS.
- The public Testnet-10 acceptance list. It stopped storing new payments on 25 Sep 2026. A lock the list does not show is read from a synced node, about the last minute of accepted blocks. A mempool-only payment is not credited.
